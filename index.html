    About this map

    Finding a good mountain-bike spot means trawling a dozen resort sites, none of which tell you what you actually need to know in one place: what the trails are like, what the lift costs, and how long it takes to get there. And once you have found somewhere, there is rarely a KML or GPX to drop into your GPS app.

    This map is that missing file. Every spot lives in a single KML you can download and load into CoMaps, Organic Maps or OsmAnd; this site is just a convenient way to browse and filter it without installing anything.

    Where the data comes from

    • Trail geometry from OpenStreetMap MTB route relations, pulled via Overpass and simplified. Indicative only: follow the signs on the ground, not the trace.
    • Lift prices, opening dates and travel times compiled from the operators' own sites and Magic Pass, in July 2026. Where a 2026 figure could not be confirmed the pin says so rather than guessing.
    • Trail descriptions written by an LLM, from riding and from operator trail maps.
    • Base map tiles from OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap (CC BY-SA) and CyclOSM.

    Contributing

    Spots are missing, and prices go stale. Add yours to public/alpine-mtb-map.kml, check it renders with vp i && vp dev, and send a pull request. The README has a template and the tag reference.
